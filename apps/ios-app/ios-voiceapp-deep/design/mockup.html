How the business labeling works

1. Sync — On first launch and on demand, the app pulls all phone numbers from your Twilio account (/IncomingPhoneNumbers). Each is saved as a Business with a name, color, and brand mark you can edit.

2. Inbound — When a call/SMS arrives, Twilio's webhook payload includes To (the number that was dialed). The app looks up the matching Business and uses its name + color on the incoming screen and in history.

3. Outbound — Before placing a call from the Keypad or pressing Send in a thread, you pick which line to use. That choice becomes the From on the Twilio API request, so caller ID matches the business.

4. History — Every Call and Message persists the businessId so Recents/Messages can filter and color-code instantly without re-querying.
